# Paper C measurement specification C6 (released build, main conditions)

Status: written and frozen 2026-11-12 after the C1 to C5 results were read and after a reviewer asked for the main
conditions to be rerun with the released build; before any C6 run. Hashes in `paper_c/prereg/MANIFEST`.
Pre-specified, not publicly registered. Because it was written after the earlier results were known, the manuscript
must say so. No smoke runs: build, geometry, guest image, run script, batch runner and analysis are those of C4, whose
smoke and formal runs used them on SC-T0 and SC-TF.

## Question
C4 compared the released trace build with C1 on 24 cells (targz and sqlite, two replicates). Does the released build
reproduce C1 in the two central conditions for every tool?

## Design
- Build, guest image (copy-on-write overlays of the C1 image, instances 3 and 4), kernel, 7168-MiB geometry,
  `exp/femu_fs.sh`, `exp/fs_map.py`, `exp3/c3_batch.sh` and `exp/analyze_c.py`: as in C4.
- Runs: ext4, XFS, Btrfs, F2FS x SC-T0, SC-TF x all C1 tools (copynr, rsync, targz, git, gzipf, sqlite; copy on XFS
  and Btrfs only) x 3 replicates = 156 runs, in a fixed random order (seed 20261113). Launcher `exp6/c6_run.sh`.

## Comparison
Per cell (52 cells), against the same C1 cell, with the margins of C2 to C5: a cell agrees if the mean overwrite share
differs by at most 5 percentage points and the mean delta ratio by at most 0.05. Also reported: for each C6 run,
whether both values lie within the range of the five C1 runs of its cell; and the number of cells in which the C1
statements of the manuscript that concern these two conditions would change if C6 replaced C1 (reported, not used to
replace C1). All 52 cells and all 156 runs are reported whatever the result. A cell that disagrees is stated as such;
the released build is not described as reproducing it.

## Validity
C1 gates G1 (unmapped pre-tool pages at most 5 %) and G4 (failed runs rerun once, tool errors reported) apply. G2 is
not informative (no retention by construction). gzipf exits with an error in every run, as in C1 (set-user-ID files
skipped); this is reported, not a failure.

## Limits to state
Three replicates; two of the five C1 conditions; written after the earlier results were known.
