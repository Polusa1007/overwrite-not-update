# Paper C measurement specification C4 (released trace build)

Status: written 2026-11-11 after the C1, C2 and C3 results were read; frozen 2026-11-11 after its 2 smoke runs passed
(build boots, C1 record format, analyze_c.py reads it; ext4 SC-TF targz 6.3 % overwrite share, delta ratio 0.14;
f2fs SC-TD sqlite 88.6 %; no design fault), before any
formal run. Hashes in `paper_c/prereg/MANIFEST`. Pre-specified, not publicly registered.

## Question
C1 to C3 used a FEMU build whose FTL also contains a page-retention extension from other, unpublished work; in the
trace mode used (`FEMU_RFTL=trace`) that extension retains no page (`retained_now` = 0 in every run) and the mapping is
updated as in stock FEMU. The artifact instead releases `femu/ftl-trace.c`, `femu/ftl-trace.h` and
`femu/apply_trace.py`, which add only the trace mode to stock FEMU (commit 2d50161f24c4cb86d58185b0efd44457412eadce).
Does the released build reproduce the C1 results?

## Design
- Build: clean clone of FEMU at that commit, `apply_trace.py`, `femu-compile.sh`; nothing else changed.
- Everything else as in C1: guest image (copy-on-write overlays on the C1 image), kernel, device geometry (7168 MiB),
  `exp/femu_fs.sh`, `exp/fs_map.py`, `exp3/c3_batch.sh` (run list as argument), `exp/analyze_c.py`.
- Runs: ext4, XFS, Btrfs, F2FS x SC-T0, SC-TD, SC-TF x targz, sqlite x 2 replicates = 48 runs, in a fixed random
  order (seed 20261111), on two emulator instances (3 and 4).

## Comparison
Per cell, against the same C1 cell: mean overwrite share and mean delta ratio. A cell agrees if the difference is at
most 5 percentage points in overwrite share and at most 0.05 in delta ratio (the margins of C2 and C3). Also reported:
for each C4 run, whether its overwrite share and delta ratio lie within the range of the five C1 runs of its cell.
All 24 cells and all 48 runs are reported whatever the result. A disagreement is stated in the manuscript and in the
artifact; the released build is then not described as reproducing that cell.

## Validity
C1 gates G1 (unmapped pre-tool pages at most 5 %) and G4 (tool errors reported) apply. G2 is not informative here: the
released build has no retention, and its dump reports `retained_now=0` by construction.

## Smoke runs
One run (ext4, SC-TF, targz, replicate 0) and one (f2fs, SC-TD, sqlite, replicate 0) to check that the build boots,
that the trace has the C1 record format and that `analyze_c.py` reads it. Not manuscript data. A fault found there is
fixed and recorded here before the formal runs.

## Limits to state
Two replicates per cell; the released build is compared with C1 on 24 of its 130 cells.
